## Dial knobs (`:root` in mock)

- `--paper-opacity` — overall paper layer strength (0–1)
- `--paper-wrinkle` — warp/skew intensity on photo layer (0–1)
- `--paper-wear` — contrast/aging on photo layer (0–1)
